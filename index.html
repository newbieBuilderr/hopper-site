Post at the right time, without watching the clock

Hopper turns a folder of videos into TikTok drafts, timed to when your own account actually performs.

What it does

Hopper is a desktop app for Windows. You give it a folder for each of your TikTok accounts. Drop a video in, and Hopper writes a caption, works out the best hour to post for that specific account, and uploads the video to that account's TikTok drafts when the time comes.

Hopper never posts anything publicly. Every video lands in your TikTok drafts, and you publish it yourself from the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. Connect your accounts. You sign in to each account through TikTok's own login screen and approve access. Hopper never sees your password.
  2. Point it at a folder. One folder per account, anywhere on your PC. Hopper watches for new video files.
  3. It learns your timing. Hopper reads the view counts and post times of your own recent videos and ranks the hours that performed best for that account.
  4. Drafts appear. At the next good hour, the video is uploaded to your TikTok drafts with a caption, and you get a desktop notification. Review it, tweak it, post it.

Why drafts, not auto-posting

Templated, high-volume posting gets throttled, and a caption written without seeing the video is a starting point, not a finished one. Hopper handles the tedious part — the file handling, the timing, the first draft of the caption — and leaves the judgement call to you.

Your data

Everything stays on your computer. There is no Hopper server, no account to create, and no analytics. See the privacy policy for the detail.
